Our story

We press.
You pip.

Pip started with one juicer, one farmers’ market stall, and a hydraulic press we bought secondhand and did not entirely know how to use. These days it’s still small-batch, still cold-pressed, still sourced from growers we can call by name. No pasteurizing, no powders, no shortcuts — just fruit, pressure, and a deliberately short shelf life.

  1. 01
    Wash

    Every piece, by hand, the same day it’s picked.

  2. 02
    Press

    Cold hydraulic press — no heat, no oxidation.

  3. 03
    Bottle

    Glass, capped, into the fridge within the hour.
